Recommendation

If you can only pick one: Gaja Alternative Asset Management (82/100, Strong Apply). It's the cleanest set of numbers in this group — margins expanding every year (43%→50%→52%), PAT growing every year with no spike, essentially no debt, and 82% of the fresh issue funding genuine growth capital (fund-sponsor commitments) rather than debt repayment or promoter exit. The catch: it hasn't opened yet (opens tomorrow, 19 Aug), so there's no live subscription or GMP trend to confirm the market agrees — the 82 reflects fundamentals, not market validation. If you want a demand signal before committing, wait a day and check Day 1 subscription before applying.

If you want a name that's already open and market-tested: Shankesh Jewellers (70, Apply) or Lalithaa Jewellery Mart (67, Apply) are the strongest of the five currently-open issues — both cheap on P/E versus peers, both closing within 24-48 hours. Shankesh scores slightly higher on valuation and growth consistency, but Lalithaa's promoter and cash-flow picture (despite the FY26 OCF concern) is somewhat better documented. Neither is a clean story — both carry a genuine cash-conversion or gold-price-sustainability question.

Tempsens Instruments (75, Apply) has the best underlying business of all seven — three and a half decades of track record, a real niche leadership position, low debt, and growth that's decelerating (not spiking) into the filing year, which is the opposite of the red flag pattern seen elsewhere in this set. It scores lower than Gaja only because 85% of the issue is OFS (promoters and PE investors cashing out, not the company raising money) and the 35.4x P/E has no peer to benchmark against. If the extremely hot GMP (~51%) cools by the time it opens on 20 Aug, this is worth a second look.

What to avoid or treat as a pure listing-gain punt only: Horizon Industrial Parks, Sunshine Pictures, and Fascinate Textiles (59, 55, 53 — all "Apply for listing gains only" at best). Horizon is loss-making with widening losses and its FY26 interest bill ate the entire operating cash flow — a genuinely large, institutionally-backed platform, but not yet a business that supports itself. Sunshine Pictures has two straight years of revenue decline and negative operating cash flow despite reporting a profit. Fascinate Textiles combines a reviewer-flagged unsustainable profit spike with zero grey-market premium and conflicting source data on even its price band — that combination (explosive growth story + zero market enthusiasm) is a real signal, not noise.

Data-quality caveats — read before acting

Gaja and Tempsens are pre-open. Their scores are built on disclosed fundamentals and a single pre-open GMP reading. No subscription data exists yet for either — that's the single biggest swing factor for both scores once they open.
Several D/E and OCF figures come from a single source (Horizon's D/E was reported as both 1.2x and 1.47x across sources; Gaja, Tempsens and Fascinate had no operating-cash-flow figure available in the sources reviewed at all — scored conservatively rather than assumed clean). Verify these against each RHP before sizing a position.
Fascinate Textiles has an unusually high rate of cross-source disagreement — price band (₹148-156 vs ₹142-151) and even the closing date are disputed across trackers. This alone is a data point about SME disclosure quality, separate from the business itself.
